    Community

    Share a mushroom you found

    Just upload a trail photo. If it has GPS, location is filled automatically. Finds are added as pending identification and can be named later when we review the gallery.

    • Keep Location Services on so the photo is geotagged
    • No need to know the species — ID can wait
    • Optional note if you want (trail name, tree, etc.)
    • Never eat a wild mushroom from a photo alone

    JPG/PNG from your phone · max ~6 MB · GPS is read when present
